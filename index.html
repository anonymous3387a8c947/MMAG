🎵 MMAG

Multi‑Control Mixed Audio Generation Benchmark · Anonymous

📄 Introduction

MMAG is a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating mixed audio generation under multiple control conditions. It consists of a Main Set for holistic evaluation, along with two dedicated subsets for voice cloning (Prompt Set) and timestamp‑conditioned generation (Timestamp Set), enabling fine‑grained assessment of speaker identity preservation, temporal alignment, and cross‑domain acoustic fidelity.

All annotations are curated through a multi‑expert pipeline with human quality control.

🔧 Construction

Annotation Pipeline
Overview of the MMAG annotation pipeline. Expert models extract fine-grained semantic, speaker, and temporal attributes from audio clips. An LLM aggregates these annotations into coherent overall and timestamped captions, while speech segments are further processed to construct voice prompts for voice cloning evaluation. Human inspection is performed to ensure annotation accuracy and quality.
Caption Example Comparison
Illustration of the two caption types in MMAG for the same audio clip. Overall captions provide a holistic description of speech, sound events, and music with their relative temporal relationships, while timestamped captions additionally specify fine-grained temporal boundaries for speech segments and foreground sound events.
