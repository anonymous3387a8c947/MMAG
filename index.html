🎵 MMAG

Multi‑Control Mixed Audio Generation Benchmark

📄 Overview

MMAG is a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating mixed audio generation under multiple control conditions. It consists of a Main Set for holistic evaluation, along with two dedicated subsets for voice cloning and timestamp‑conditioned generation, enabling fine‑grained assessment of speaker identity preservation, temporal alignment, and cross‑domain acoustic fidelity.

The benchmark provides rich annotations covering speech content, speaker attributes, music metadata, sound events, and temporal relationships, curated through a multi‑expert annotation pipeline with human quality control.

📊 Benchmark Statistics

Main Set ~4,000 clips Voice Cloning ~690 clips Timestamp ~1,800 clips Total ~6,500 clips
speech music sound effects temporal boundaries speaker identity

All clips are sourced from AudioCaps, VGGSound, and MECAT.
